# Adversarial proof review

1. **Parity and definition.** Ordered pairs of undirected graphs give half-factors of an even-degree support. The converse upper comparison explicitly requires even total degree. Without it the directed triangle is a counterexample. Opposite arcs are allowed in D but not in O. Empty supports, isolated vertices, forced degree zero and n-1 are included. Every local c_i <= both d_i and n-1-d_i.
2. **Transition systems.** Use edge ends, not vertex-simple cycles. Pairings induce trails without edge repetition; repeated vertices do not affect two traversal/colour choices. Incoming-to-outgoing matching and red-to-blue matching each have c_i! choices. Half-factor <= Eulerian orientation is known. For even components, restrict the count to single-tour systems only: each such system is even and supports equal orientation/colour multiplicities. Never assume all pairings of a half-factor have one tour.
3. **Last exit.** Weak connectivity plus balance implies directed reachability. Fix the root, first arc and in-arborescence. Outgoing order at the root has first arc fixed, others have last arc fixed. If a traversal closes early, unused arcs are balanced; last-exit tree paths imply unused root out-degree, contradiction. Starting at the fixed arc uniquely recovers every order, eliminating cyclic overcount. The full BEST theorem is prior; the proof here needs its lower bound only.
4. **Parity switching.** Cross pairs are either empty or double, never single, as the original O-components are distinct. Odd torus periods force equal cross types. The empty and full operations preserve both directed margins and binary legality. In both cases the same two single arcs are replaced. Each deleted arc leaves an Euler trail between its endpoints; the new cross arcs concatenate the trails, so no vertices are lost and the resulting union is a single even component. Because the paired unions are disjoint, no later switch changes an already repaired component.
5. **Inverse code.** Mark only the final component's two inserted single arcs (unordered) and one bit. For the full switch the reverse arcs were inserted into old single pairs; decode with the full output adjacency data. Each inverse is unique; no split/order information is needed. Counting invalid codewords is harmless. A merged component has s>=6, and 1+l(l-1)<=s^4<=4^s. Multiply over disjoint components, not over arbitrary global choices. No original endpoint label outside the marked final component is needed. No assumption of uniform repair fibres.
6. **AM-GM.** For positive d use P <= product d_i <= (2M/n)^n; (2+x)^2>=8x yields C=1. C is an admissible constant, not a claimed sharp one. The product formulation handles zeros; odd total remains excluded.
7. **Consequences.** The entropy estimate is borrowed from 2611.02191, Theorems 4.2 and 6.1. For zero degrees remove forced isolates before using this bound; the product estimate with original n is weaker and remains valid. For cut norm, symmetric entries have weights 0/1/2 on each independent unordered edge; use variance <=4M, summands bounded by 2, 4^n ordered cuts and conditioning cost e^K. No assertion of independence under the conditioned law. The older tree-statistic reduction uses the older paper's notation and assumptions. Neither consequence claims a sharper asymptotic enumeration formula.
8. **Scope and tests.** Complete simple host is essential to repair; forbidden edge hosts are not covered. The much stronger polynomial-loss comparison is open in this work. The finite checks are overlapping diagnostics, not a proof of global novelty or of the universal theorem. The Lean formalization addresses parity, integer margin-preserving updates, inversion, and scalar inequalities only. Combinatorial incidence counting and graph-limit corollaries remain analytical.
